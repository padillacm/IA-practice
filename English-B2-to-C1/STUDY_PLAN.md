# 16-Week Intensive Plan: B2 → C1

**Target:** 10–12 hours/week · **Focus:** advanced grammar, vocabulary/collocations, listening, speaking · **Goal:** real-world C1 (no exam)

Cambridge English estimates about **200 guided learning hours** to move up one CEFR level. At 11 hours a week this plan adds
up to about 175 hours. Add the extra listening and conversation you do for fun, and you get past 200.
The plan is demanding but realistic. **Consistency matters more than intensity**, so 90 minutes every day beats 10 hours on Sunday.

---

## The weekly template (~11 h)

| Block | When | Time | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔁 **Flashcards** | every day | 15 min | Notebook 08: due reviews + 10 new cards |
| 📘 **Core study** | Mon–Fri | 30–40 min | The notebook for the current phase (grammar/vocabulary) |
| 🎧 **Intensive listening** | Mon–Fri | 20 min | Notebook 06: a dictation **or** a 5-pass clip |
| 🗣️ **Speaking drill** | Mon–Fri | 15 min | Notebook 07: shadowing (Mon/Wed/Fri) · 4-3-2 timed talk (Tue/Thu) |
| 🎬 **Extensive listening** | any time | 3–4 h/week | Podcasts, series and YouTube for pleasure (commute, gym, cooking) |
| 💬 **Real conversation** | 2×/week | 2 × 30–60 min | italki/Preply tutor, a language exchange, or an AI partner |
| 📊 **Weekly review** | Sunday | 45 min | Notebook 09: log hours, `weekly_report()`, `review_mistakes()`, redo weak sets |

### A typical weekday (≈ 90 min)
```
07:30  15'  Flashcards (phone/Anki or Notebook 08)
08:00  30'  Podcast on the commute (extensive): no pausing, just enjoy
13:30  20'  Dictation or intensive clip (Notebook 06)
19:00  35'  Notebook of the phase: one section + exercise + check()
19:35  15'  Shadowing or a timed speaking prompt (record yourself!)
       +1'  log_study(...)
```

## Phase 1: Foundations (Weeks 1–4)
*Goal: know exactly where you stand; master conditionals and inversion; start the daily habits.*

| Week | Core study (Mon–Fri) | Listening focus | Speaking focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NB00 diagnostic**, then NB01 §1 (conditionals) | NB06 §1 connected speech + dictations 1–2 | Functional language bank (NB07 §1): learn 2 categories |
| 2 | NB01 §2 (wishes) + §3 (inversion) | Dictations 3–4 · BBC *6 Minute English* | Shadow one TED-Ed clip all week |
| 3 | NB01 §4 (clefts) + §5 review · NB03 §1 (verb+noun) | Passages 1–2 (NB06 §3) · first worksheet | 4-3-2 technique, 3 prompts |
| 4 | NB03 §2–3 (adj/adverb collocations, ideas & data) | Dictations 5–6 · *The Indicator* / *Planet Money* | First recorded self-assessment |

✅ **End of week 4:** retake the diagnostic (NB00) and fill in the history table in NB09.

## Phase 2: Building range (Weeks 5–8)
*Goal: past modals, passives and participle clauses; phrasal verbs; drop the subtitles.*

| Week | Core study | Listening focus | Speaking focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | NB02 §1 (past modals) + §2 (passives, causative) | Passages 3–4 · series with **English** subs | Pronunciation (NB07 §2): -ed endings, word stress |
| 6 | NB02 §3–4 (participle clauses, emphasis) | Dictations 7–8 (accent `co.uk`) | Minimal pairs + tongue twisters · shadowing |
| 7 | NB02 §5–6 · NB04 §1–2 (particles, formal→phrasal) | 3 worksheets: *Hidden Brain*, TED, a YouTube interview | Workplace prompts (NB07 §4) |
| 8 | NB04 §3–4 (register, idioms) · NB03 §4 upgrade | One series episode **without subtitles** | A 10-min "presentation" on your job, recorded |

✅ **End of week 8:** retake the diagnostic and the C1 can-do checklist (NB09 §5).

## Phase 3: Flexibility & precision (Weeks 9–12)
*Goal: paraphrase anything; precise vocabulary; understand fast, unscripted speech.*

| Week | Core study | Listening focus | Speaking focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | NB05 §1 (word formation) | Dictations 9–10 (accent `com.au` / `ie`) | Daily paraphrasing drill (NB05 "Your turn") |
| 10 | NB05 §2 (key word transformations) | *Freakonomics* / *99% Invisible*, no transcript | Discussion prompts: argue both sides |
| 11 | NB05 §3 (confusables, false friends) · redo every set < 85% | Panel shows / fast podcasts · 2 accents new to you | Storytelling prompts using inversion + clefts |
| 12 | Redo NB01–02 error-correction sets · `review_mistakes()` daily | *In Our Time* (BBC Radio 4) | Mock "interview" or negotiation with a tutor |

✅ **End of week 12:** retake the diagnostic.

## Phase 4: Consolidation & real-world use (Weeks 13–16)
*Goal: make everything automatic under pressure.*

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 13 | Spend most of your time on output: 3 conversations this week. After each one, write down 3 "gaps" (things you couldn't say) and add them to `my_words` |
| 14 | Consume native content only (no learner materials) for a week: a book or long reads, a series, podcasts. Keep a chunk notebook |
| 15 | Give a 10-minute talk in English (Toastmasters, work, or recorded) and get feedback. Redo all sets < 90% |
| 16 | **Final diagnostic** + can-do checklist + an optional free external check (EF SET) or a Cambridge C1 sample test for a benchmark. Plan the next 16 weeks toward C1+/C2 |

## Rules that make the difference
1. **Record yourself** at least 3 times a week. It's uncomfortable, and it's the fastest feedback loop you have.
2. **Chunks, not words.** Every new word goes into a flashcard with its collocation and an example.
3. **Mistakes are data.** The mistake bank (`review_mistakes()`) is your personal syllabus.
4. **Output every day.** Say every example aloud, and use each new structure in a sentence about *your* life.
5. **Enjoy the extensive part.** Pick podcasts and series you'd watch anyway. Hours of input that you actually enjoy are the fuel.
6. **Missed a day?** Just do flashcards + 10 min of listening. Never miss two days in a row.

## If you have less time some weeks
Minimum viable day (30 min): flashcards 10' + dictation or podcast 10' + one speaking prompt 10'.
