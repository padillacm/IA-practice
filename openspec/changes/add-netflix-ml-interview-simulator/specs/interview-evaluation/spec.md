# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Produces an independent, verifiable and actionable evaluation of each stage, and simulates the cut between phases and the debrief with the hiring manager's decision, including a study plan to close the gaps.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Competency-based rubric
Each stage SHALL have a versioned rubric with 3 to 6 competencies, one of them a culture competency tied to memo values. Each competency SHALL have a stable id, a flag for whether it is core, and an observable "Yes" bar per level L4, L5 and L6. Strong No, No and Strong Yes are defined relative to that bar.

#### Scenario: Valid rubric
- **WHEN** the content is validated
- **THEN** each rubric has a version; 3–6 competencies with unique ids and at least one core; the culture one with its memo values; and L4/L5/L6 bars distinct from one another, with no empty adjectives

#### Scenario: Version change
- **WHEN** a rubric changes version
- **THEN** its competency ids are kept, or declared renamed

### Requirement: Independent, fact-based evaluation
The scorecard of each instance SHALL be generated by an evaluator agent distinct from the interviewer. The evaluator SHALL receive only the transcript without the interviewer's private notes, the configuration and the rubric. With immediate feedback it is evaluated before continuing; with deferred feedback, in the background.

#### Scenario: Private notes
- **WHEN** the interviewer wrote judgments in its private field
- **THEN** the copy the evaluator receives does not contain them

### Requirement: Verifiable evidence
Each score SHALL be backed by at least one verbatim quote from a candidate turn, identified by its turn number. The system SHALL verify that each quote appears literally in that turn before showing the scorecard.

#### Scenario: Nonexistent quote
- **WHEN** a quote does not appear in the indicated candidate turn
- **THEN** the system asks the evaluator to correct it once and, if it still does not appear, that competency becomes "no evidence" with a warning in the scorecard

### Requirement: Pedagogical recommendation rule
The recommendation for each stage SHALL be derived from the scores with a fixed rule:
- **fail**: any core competency at Strong No, or two or more at No.
- **insufficient signal**: any core without evidence.
- **pass**: in all other cases.

The observed level is the highest one whose Yes bar is met by all core competencies. The scorecard SHALL state: "Recommendation derived by the simulator's pedagogical rule; Netflix does not use a company-wide scale."

#### Scenario: Two core at No
- **WHEN** two core competencies have No and the rest Yes
- **THEN** the recommendation is fail

#### Scenario: No evidence
- **WHEN** a core competency has no evidence
- **THEN** the recommendation is insufficient signal, which counts as neither pass nor fail

### Requirement: Effect of hints and stage end
A nudge-type hint SHALL cap the affected competency at Yes, and a substantive one at No; neither affects other competencies. If the stage ends from an external cause, uncovered competencies stay "no evidence". If it ends from a candidate cause (time ran out or points left uncovered despite redirection), what was missing SHALL be scored and listed.

#### Scenario: Substantive hint
- **WHEN** the candidate needed a substantive hint to advance on a competency
- **THEN** that competency cannot exceed No and the scorecard explains it in a hints section

#### Scenario: Time exhausted by the candidate
- **WHEN** the candidate ran out the time of `ml-system-design` without covering monitoring
- **THEN** the corresponding competency is scored and the scorecard lists the uncovered points

### Requirement: Scorecard content
The scorecard SHALL include, in the session language:
- the score of each competency with its evidence;
- for each one below Yes, what a Yes-level answer would look like;
- the recommendation, the observed level, the strengths and the gaps;
- a study plan whose first 1–3 items are the recommendations.

Its structured header SHALL use English enums and the rubric version.

#### Scenario: Justification with evidence
- **WHEN** the evaluator scores No on a competency
- **THEN** the scorecard quotes the turn that justifies it and explains what a Yes answer would have been

### Requirement: Feedback at the end of each stage
The system SHALL show the scorecard of each stage when it ends, except in deferred mode, in which it is saved and shown together with the final decision. Before the cut and the debrief, it SHALL wait for all the necessary scorecards to exist.

#### Scenario: Deferred feedback
- **WHEN** the candidate enabled deferred feedback
- **THEN** at the cut they see only the decision, and they see the scorecards at the end

### Requirement: Per-role weights
The catalog SHALL assign each stage of each loop a weight (high, normal or low) used by the debrief. For example, for `mle`, `ml-system-design`, `culture` and `ml-depth` weigh more, and `coding` less. For `ds`, the experimentation stages, `stats` and `culture` weigh more.

#### Scenario: Missing weight
- **WHEN** a stage of a default loop has no weight
- **THEN** content validation fails

### Requirement: Cut debrief after VO1
For roles with an active cut, a debrief agent SHALL decide whether to advance to VO2 using the final debrief rule, applied to the existing scorecards. If there are no evaluated screens, it says so.

#### Scenario: Advance to VO2
- **WHEN** all the high-weight VO1 stages have pass
- **THEN** the cut decides to continue and communicates it in a recruiter's tone

#### Scenario: Negative cut
- **WHEN** a high-weight VO1 stage has fail
- **THEN** the cut decides not to continue, justifying it with references to the scorecards

### Requirement: Debrief and hiring manager decision
With two or more completed instances, a debrief agent SHALL compute a provisional outcome with a fixed rule. It will then simulate the panel discussion: each interviewer's pass or fail, "would anyone change their mind?" citing only existing evidence, and consensus seeking. Finally, the HM decides as informed captain and may depart from the provisional outcome only by justifying it with scorecard quotes.

#### Scenario: Firm fail in a high-weight stage
- **WHEN** a high-weight stage has a core competency at Strong No and the rest is positive
- **THEN** the provisional outcome is No Hire and, if the HM changes it, the decision marks it as an override with its justification

#### Scenario: Mixed signals
- **WHEN** an `mle` has Strong Yes in `ml-system-design` and fail in `coding`
- **THEN** the outcome follows the majority weighted by the role's weights and the debrief explains that calculation

#### Scenario: Good candidate, another team
- **WHEN** the high-weight VO1 stages are pass and culture is positive, but team fit is weak (fails only in `ml-depth` or `hm-onsite`)
- **THEN** the decision is "not a fit for this team, referred" and it offers to repeat VO2 with a different domain

#### Scenario: Hire below the target level
- **WHEN** the outcome is Hire but the level observed in the high-weight stages is below the target
- **THEN** the decision is Hire at the observed level, and the debrief explains that it is a pedagogical result

#### Scenario: Incomplete loop or screens only
- **WHEN** the debrief is convened with stages not done, or with screens only
- **THEN** the decision is marked provisional and lists what is missing

#### Scenario: Practice after a negative cut
- **WHEN** VO2 was done in practice mode after a negative cut
- **THEN** the final decision keeps the No Hire from the cut, and the practice stages only contribute feedback and study plan

### Requirement: Culture aggregated by value
The debrief SHALL aggregate the culture signals from all stages by memo value, into a single culture verdict, without counting the same weakness once per stage.

#### Scenario: Repeated weakness
- **WHEN** three stages record a weak signal for the same value
- **THEN** the culture verdict treats it as a single weakness backed by three pieces of evidence

### Requirement: Linked study plan
The study plan SHALL prioritize the gaps and link the `recsys-course/` module assigned to each competency. When the course does not cover the topic, it SHALL use the map's alternative: a stage to practice or a resource such as the culture memo.

#### Scenario: Gap in experimentation
- **WHEN** the `experimentation` scorecard points to a gap in variance reduction
- **THEN** the plan links `recsys-course/15_online_experimentation/`

#### Scenario: Gap without a module
- **WHEN** the gap is in coding or SQL
- **THEN** the plan proposes practicing the corresponding stage, instead of a course module

### Requirement: Verifiable evaluator quality
The project SHALL include a set of reference transcripts with expected results, to manually check the consistency of the evaluator and the debrief.

#### Scenario: Consistency
- **WHEN** the same reference transcript is scored three times
- **THEN** the recommendation matches across all three and no competency varies by more than one point
