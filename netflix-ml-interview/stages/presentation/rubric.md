# Rubric: presentation

L5 is the anchor level; L4 and L6 bars are extrapolated from it. Netflix publishes no common scale. Applies to
both variants (`research-talk`, `take-home`).

## Shared definitions
- **Yes**: the observable behavior in the level's row of the competency table occurs in the candidate's own words or materials.
- **No**: the candidate reaches part of the bar (some elements present, one or more missing) or reaches it only after a nudge.
- **Strong No**: the candidate contradicts the bar (for example reports an effect without any uncertainty, or cannot explain their own method) or needed a substantive hint.
- **Strong Yes**: the candidate meets the Yes bar of the next level up, with specific evidence.
- No evidence: the topic never came up for a reason outside the candidate's control.

## communication_structure
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | States the problem, the approach and one result in order, and finishes inside the allotted time. |
| L5 | Opens with the question and the decision it informs, presents method, result and limits in that order, uses one figure or table per key claim, and gives the takeaway in two sentences when asked. |
| L6 | Frames the problem for a mixed audience, adjusts depth after a panel interruption without losing the thread, and closes with the decision, the open risks and the next step with an owner. |

## technical_depth_rigor
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Names the baseline and one assumption, and reports a result with a number from a comparison. |
| L5 | States the baseline, tests or lists the main assumptions, reports uncertainty (interval or variance across runs), and explains why one alternative was rejected. |
| L6 | Defines the evaluation approach, quantifies a limitation of the method (for example a bias or an extrapolation risk), and proposes an analysis that would resolve the main open question. |

## impact_and_insight
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Links the result to one product metric or user outcome and states what it means for the team. |
| L5 | Names the decision or metric that changed, separates their contribution from others', and states a guardrail or cost alongside the gain. |
| L6 | Quantifies impact beyond one team or time window, explains adoption by other teams, and states what they would stop doing because of the result. |

## qa_handling
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Answers the question that was asked, and says "I do not know" when applicable instead of guessing. |
| L5 | Answers directly in the first sentence, concedes a valid limitation without defending it, and proposes a way to find the missing answer. |
| L6 | Redirects a hostile or ambiguous question into the underlying concern, offers a concrete test or data pull, and updates the recommendation in the room if the panel's point is valid. |

## culture_signals
core: false
memo_values: [candor, curiosity, courage]

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Mentions a limitation or failed attempt in their own work without prompting. |
| L5 | Names a result that did not work, what was learned, and credits collaborators by name. |
| L6 | Disagrees with a panel claim with evidence, politely, and asks the panel a question that shows curiosity about their approach. |
