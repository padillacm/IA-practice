# Rubric: hm-screen

L5 is the anchor level; L4 and L6 bars are extrapolated from it. Netflix publishes no common scale.

## Shared definitions
- **Yes**: the observable behavior in the level's row of the competency table occurs in the candidate's own words.
- **No**: the candidate partly reaches the bar (elements present, one or more missing) or reaches it only after a nudge.
- **Strong No**: the candidate contradicts the bar (for example cannot describe what they personally did, or the
  background does not cover the role) or needed a substantive hint.
- **Strong Yes**: the candidate meets the Yes bar of the next level up, with specific evidence.
- No evidence: the topic never came up for a reason outside the candidate's control.

## role_background_fit
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes one project in their own domain with their personal contribution, tools used and the data or users involved; connects it to at least one need of the team. |
| L5 | Describes owning a system or problem area end to end, names the scale (users, requests, data size or experiments), and maps at least two of their skills to named needs of the team. |
| L6 | Describes setting direction across more than one team or system, names who they had to align, and maps their experience to the team's roadmap risks rather than to tools. |

## impact_with_metrics
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Gives one outcome with a number (offline metric or business metric) and the baseline it was compared to. |
| L5 | Gives at least two outcomes with metric, baseline and how it was measured (online test or equivalent), and says what they personally changed to move it. |
| L6 | Gives outcomes at team or business level with metric, baseline and measurement method, states a case where the result was neutral or negative, and what decision followed. |

## technical_curiosity
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Names something they learned in the last six months and asks at least one "how does the team do X" question. |
| L5 | Asks why the team chose its approach over an alternative and names a technique or paper they tried and what happened. |
| L6 | Connects a technical trend to a decision the team would face (build, buy, retire) and asks what evidence would change the team's mind. |

## candidate_questions_quality
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Asks at least two questions about the team's work, tooling or how success is measured. |
| L5 | Asks about the problem the team will not solve this year, how decisions are made and how impact is evaluated for this role. |
| L6 | Asks about cross-team interfaces, what the team has decided to stop doing, and what the hiring manager would want fixed in the first six months. |

## culture_signals
core: false
memo_values: [candor, curiosity, judgment]

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes one instance of asking for or acting on feedback, with the change they made. |
| L5 | Describes a disagreement with a stakeholder, states their position and the outcome, and credits the other party's input. |
| L6 | Describes a decision they made with incomplete data, who they consulted, and what they wrote down afterwards about what they learned. |
