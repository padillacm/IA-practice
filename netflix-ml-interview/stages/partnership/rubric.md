# Rubric: partnership

L5 is the anchor level; L4 and L6 bars are extrapolated from it. Netflix publishes no common scale.

## Shared definitions
- **Yes**: the observable behavior in the level's row of the competency table occurs in the candidate's own words,
  for a specific past event.
- **No**: the candidate partly reaches the bar (elements present, one or more missing) or reaches it only after a nudge.
- **Strong No**: the candidate contradicts the bar (for example blames the partner team, or escalated without trying
  to resolve it first) or needed a substantive hint.
- **Strong Yes**: the candidate meets the Yes bar of the next level up, with specific evidence.
- No evidence: the topic never came up for a reason outside the candidate's control.

## cross_team_collaboration
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes one project with another team, the interface or handoff they agreed, and what the other team needed from them. |
| L5 | Describes leading work that needed two or more teams, how they discovered each team's goals, how ownership and interfaces were agreed and documented, and the outcome. |
| L6 | Describes establishing a recurring mechanism among several teams (shared roadmap review, interface contract or on-call agreement), the problem it solved and evidence it was adopted. |

## stakeholder_influence
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes persuading a stakeholder with data, framed for the stakeholder's goal, and the result. |
| L5 | Describes a disagreement with a stakeholder, states each side's position, the evidence used, and the outcome; includes either a changed position of their own or a commitment to a decision they opposed. |
| L6 | Describes aligning leaders with conflicting goals, the trade-off they made explicit, the dissent they sought, and how the decision was recorded and executed. |

## dependency_conflict_handling
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes a dependency that slipped, when they noticed and whom they told. |
| L5 | Describes a slipped dependency, the options they offered (scope, date, resources), what they negotiated and how they kept the partner informed. |
| L6 | Describes competing priorities across teams, how they made trade-offs visible to decision-makers and the resolution, including a case where their team lost. |

## communication_clarity
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Answers in a clear order (situation, action, result) and names the ask or decision when relevant. |
| L5 | Opens with the conclusion, gives the context needed for a partner to act, states the decision and deadline, and keeps each answer under about two minutes. |
| L6 | Adapts the message to the audience (peer, leader, executive) with an example of each, and states what they would write down so that the message outlasts the meeting. |

## culture_signals
core: false
memo_values: [selflessness, candor, inclusion, judgment]

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Credits a partner for part of a result by role and says what they learned from them. |
| L5 | Describes a time they gave up a team preference for a company outcome, and delivered unwelcome news to a partner directly. |
| L6 | Describes how they created context for partners rather than controlling them, a case where they changed their team's plan on partner input, and a bias or blind spot they corrected. |
