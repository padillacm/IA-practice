# Rubric: hm-onsite

L5 is the anchor level; L4 and L6 bars are extrapolated from it. Netflix publishes no common scale.

## Shared definitions
- **Yes**: the observable behavior in the level's row of the competency table occurs in the candidate's own words.
- **No**: the candidate partly reaches the bar (elements present, one or more missing) or reaches it only after a nudge.
- **Strong No**: the candidate contradicts the bar (for example states an incorrect domain claim and defends it
  against evidence, or cannot name a miss) or needed a substantive hint.
- **Strong Yes**: the candidate meets the Yes bar of the next level up, with specific evidence.
- No evidence: the topic never came up for a reason outside the candidate's control.

## team_domain_fit
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Explains the core mechanics of the domain problem, names two constraints specific to it, and links one past project to it. |
| L5 | Frames the domain problem from objectives to evaluation, names the hardest part and a plausible approach with a trade-off, and links two past results to the team's needs. |
| L6 | Identifies where the domain's standard approach breaks, proposes an alternative with an evaluation plan, and states what evidence would change their recommendation. |

## technical_vision_scope
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Proposes a first project with a measurable goal, the main risk and a plan to validate it. |
| L5 | Lays out a one-year plan with prioritized bets, names what they would not do and why, and defines success metrics and a validation path. |
| L6 | Lays out a multi-team plan with dependencies, sequencing and owners, states the decisions that need alignment, and names how they would test the plan's riskiest assumption early. |

## cross_team_leadership_scope
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes one collaboration with another team and what they contributed to it. |
| L5 | Describes leading a piece of work that needed another team's input, how they obtained it and the outcome. |
| L6 | Describes changing another team's priorities or process without authority, names the objection raised and how it was resolved, and the result at org level. |

## motivation_and_growth
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Names a specific skill they want to build here and how this team gives them a way to build it. |
| L5 | Names the scope they want to own in a year, what feedback they have received that shaped it, and what they are doing about it. |
| L6 | Names the organizational problem they want to own, the sponsor they would need, and one area where they expect to need to learn from others. |

## culture_signals
core: false
memo_values: [candor, courage, judgment, selflessness]

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Updates a stated position when given a counterargument, or defends it with evidence, without becoming defensive. |
| L5 | Admits a mistake from a past project with its cause and what changed, and states a disagreement with a manager or peer plainly. |
| L6 | Describes a decision they reversed or a risk they took publicly, with the outcome, and names who they credited or helped in the process. |
