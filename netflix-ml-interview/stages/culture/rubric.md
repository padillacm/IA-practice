# Rubric: culture

L5 is the anchor level; L4 and L6 bars are extrapolated from it. Netflix publishes no common scale. Only the 3 or 4
values sampled in the session are scored; unsampled values are not penalized. Across stages, the culture result is
aggregated by memo value, not by summing per stage.

## Shared definitions
- **Yes**: the observable behavior in the level's row of the competency table occurs in the candidate's own words,
  for a specific past event (who, when, what they did, result).
- **No**: the candidate gives a general principle instead of an event, omits the result or their personal part, or
  reaches the bar only after a nudge.
- **Strong No**: the candidate contradicts the bar (blames others for a failure, describes avoiding feedback,
  claims not to have read the memo and shows no interest) or needed a substantive hint.
- **Strong Yes**: the candidate meets the Yes bar of the next level up, with specific evidence.
- No evidence: the topic never came up for a reason outside the candidate's control.

## culture_signals
core: true
memo_values: [selflessness, judgment, candor, creativity, courage, inclusion, curiosity, resilience]

Score by the sampled values using the memo's descriptions: Selflessness (humility to seek the best ideas, what is best
for Netflix, helping others succeed), Judgment (long-term solutions, decisions despite ambiguity, data informing
intuition), Candor (giving and receiving feedback, admitting mistakes and sharing what was learned), Creativity (new
ideas, persisting toward more innovative solutions), Courage (vulnerability in pursuit of truth, risking failure or
challenging the status quo), Inclusion (recognizing and countering one's own biases), Curiosity (learning fast,
interest in others' ideas, humility about what one does not know), Resilience (adapting quickly, hard decisions
without delay, embracing challenges). Also scores memo literacy.

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | For each sampled value, gives one specific event from their own work with their action and the result; names two memo values correctly and uses at least one memo term (keeper test, context not control, informed captain, farming for dissent, disagree then commit) accurately. |
| L5 | For each sampled value, gives a specific event with stakes, a counterexample or cost they absorbed, and what changed afterward; connects at least two events to memo mechanisms and states one reservation about the memo. |
| L6 | For each sampled value, gives an event at team or organization scale and names a mechanism or habit they built so it repeats; contrasts memo mechanisms with a trade-off they have managed, such as loose coupling versus alignment. |

## impact_with_metrics
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes a project with a baseline, a result with a number, and their personal contribution in one sentence. |
| L5 | Describes the favorite, latest and most significant project (or two of them) with metric, baseline, measurement method and personal decisions; states what they are looking for next and why. |
| L6 | Describes outcomes at business or organization level with metric, baseline and measurement, separates their effect from the team's, and states a result that fell short and its cost. |

## high_stakes_judgment
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Describes a decision with real consequence at team scale, the information they had and lacked, and what they chose. |
| L5 | Describes a high-risk decision with incomplete data, names the options, the criteria and who they consulted, takes accountability for the outcome and states what they learned. |
| L6 | Describes a decision with high risk for people or business at org scale, names the long-term versus short-term trade-off, how they limited downside, and the post-decision review they ran. |

## candor_and_disagreement
core: true

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Gives one example of giving direct feedback to a peer and one of receiving it, with the action taken. |
| L5 | Gives an example of disagreeing with a more senior person with their reasoning, and of committing to a decision they opposed, plus how they shared a mistake of their own with others. |
| L6 | Gives an example of delivering unwelcome feedback upward or to a peer leader, describes how they sought dissent before a decision (farming for dissent) and acted as or supported an informed captain, with the outcome. |

## candidate_questions
core: false

| Level | Yes bar |
|---|---|
| L4 | Asks at least two questions that connect to what was discussed (team, expectations, how feedback works). |
| L5 | Asks about how the director handles disagreement, what success looks like at this level, or what the memo does not say about the team's day-to-day. |
| L6 | Asks about trade-offs the organization is currently carrying, how decisions are escalated, and how the director measures their own keeper test. |
