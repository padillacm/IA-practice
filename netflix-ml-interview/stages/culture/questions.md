# Question bank: culture

Prompts for the director-led Dream Team interview, built from the memo's values and mechanisms (research.md 2 Culture) and from the reported self-promotion structure (favorite, latest, most significant project; what you look for next). Each `value:` key names the memo value the question samples. Original prompts, not recalled questions.

### culture-selflessness-01
```yaml
roles: [mle, rs, ds]
levels: [L4, L5, L6]
variant: default
domain: any
kind: main
value: selflessness
signal_points:
  - credits others by name
  - chose team or company outcome over own
  - helped someone succeed with visible cost to self
```
Tell me about a time you gave up something, such as credit, scope or a preferred design, because it was better for the company or for another team.

Follow-ups:
- What did it cost you personally?
- Who benefited and how do you know?
- Did anyone know you had made that choice?
